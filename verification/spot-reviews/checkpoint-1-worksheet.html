AtomiTron fact check: checkpoint 1

Checked byDate

These 25 facts were picked at random from AtomiTron. Your job is to check each one against its source, the way a scientist checks a reference.

  1. Open AtomiTron on the iPad and go to the element. Find the fact in the section named on the card. It should match what's printed here.
  2. Tap the source button next to it. If the page opens at the top, scroll to the section with the same name.
  3. Tick one box. Matches if the source says the same thing. Doesn't match if it says something different. Can't tell if you can't find it or aren't sure: that's a perfectly good answer.
  4. Write anything you noticed in the notes.
